FAQ

Good to know.

How does it work? +

You tell us who the book is for, then share one simple link with their friends and family. Everyone can add a memory, message or photograph. We bring everything together, create the book and arrange delivery.

How many people can contribute? +

As many as you like. Whether it's five people or fifty, everyone can contribute through the same link.

Do people need to sign up? +

No. There are no accounts to create and nothing to download. They simply open the link, add their memory and submit it.

Can people add photos? +

Yes. Contributors can add a photograph alongside their memory or message.

How long does it take? +

Once all the memories are in and the book is approved, printing and delivery usually takes around 7–10 days. We'll work backwards from your date to make sure there's enough time.

What occasions is it suitable for? +

Birthdays, retirements, anniversaries, weddings, milestones and memorials — or any occasion where you want to bring everyone's memories together.

Can I order extra copies? +

Yes — just get in touch once the book is compiled and we'll arrange additional printed copies.

Do you deliver across the UK? +

Yes, delivery anywhere in the UK is included in the price.
